## 3. Detailed Scientific Figure Descriptions

### Figure A: Training and Validation Learning Curves
- **Panel (a) Loss Convergence:** Displays training and validation Focal Loss over the 5 recorded training epochs. Training loss dropped monotonically from 0.1536 to 0.0356. Validation loss declined from 0.0759 (Epoch 1) to a plateau at Epoch 3 (0.0830), before reaching 0.1206 at Epoch 4 and rising sharply to 0.1412 at Epoch 5.
- **Panel (b) Classification Accuracy:** Training accuracy climbs steadily from 79.35% to 97.31%. Validation accuracy peaks at 96.12% at Epoch 4 before dropping to 89.15% at Epoch 5.
- **Panel (c) Discriminative Metrics:** Validation F1-score and ROC-AUC plotted per epoch. Peak Validation F1-score (0.9587) and Validation Accuracy (96.12%) occurred at Epoch 4, confirming Epoch 4 as the optimal early stopping checkpoint.
- **Evidence of Overfitting:** The divergent loss trajectories at Epoch 5 (training loss continues downward to 0.0356 while validation loss climbs $+17.1\%$ to 0.1412) provide empirical evidence of overfitting onset, justifying termination.

### Figure B: Dual-Annotated Confusion Matrix
- **Sample Distribution:** Evaluated on all 129 held-out validation clips (8 unseen video sequences).
- **Class Definitions:** 
  - Non-Fall (ADL) [Class 0]: Standing, walking, sitting, bending, lying on sofa/bed ($N = 66$).
  - Fall Event [Class 1]: Loss of balance, downward vertical acceleration, kinetic impact, resting on floor ($N = 63$).
- **Counts:**
  - True Negatives (TN): 66 (100.0% class recall)
  - False Positives (FP): 0 (0.0% false alarm rate)
  - False Negatives (FN): 5 (7.94% missed fall rate)
  - True Positives (TP): 58 (92.06% class recall)
- **Derived Metrics:**
  - Accuracy: $96.12\%$
  - Precision (PPV): $100.00\%$
  - Recall / Sensitivity: $92.06\%$
  - Specificity (TNR): $100.00\%$
  - F1-Score: $0.9587$

### Figure C: Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) Curve
- **Continuous Calibration:** Plotted using genuine posterior probabilities $P(\text{Fall} \mid \mathbf{X}) \in [0.0, 1.0]$ across all 129 validation clips.
- **ROC-AUC:** Computed as **0.9817** using `sklearn.metrics.roc_auc_score`.
- **Optimal Threshold Annotation:** Youden's $J$-statistic ($J = \text{TPR} - \text{FPR}$) identifies an optimal operating point at $\tau^* = 0.666$, yielding $92.1\%$ Sensitivity and $100.0\%$ Specificity.
- **Chance Baseline:** Shown via dashed diagonal ($y = x$, $\text{AUC} = 0.5000$).

### Figure D: Precision-Recall Curve
- **AP Score:** Average Precision computed as **0.9871** via `sklearn.metrics.average_precision_score`.
- **Prevalence Baseline:** Marked at $48.84\%$ ($63 / 129$), representing the no-skill random guessing rate.
- **Iso-F1 Contours:** Plotted at $F_1 \in \{0.70, 0.80, 0.90, 0.95\}$ to visualize precision-recall tradeoffs under medical class imbalance.

### Figure E: Fall Probability Over Time (Continuous Stream Simulation)
- **Methodology:** Sliding 16-frame buffer ($T=16$, $224 \times 224$ RGB) evaluated continuously across consecutive frames at native 30 FPS camera rate.
- **Sequence (a) Fall Stream (`fall-07-cam0-rgb`):** Unseen validation fall sequence. The model outputs baseline probability $< 0.15$ during normal pre-fall walking ($0.0$--$3.0$ s). At $t = 3.17$ s, downward kinetic momentum triggers a steep transition past the warning threshold ($\tau = 0.30$) and emergency threshold ($\tau = 0.50$), reaching $p_t > 0.95$ through the post-fall lying posture.
- **Sequence (b) Non-Fall ADL Stream (`adl-04-cam0-rgb`):** Unseen validation ADL sequence. The model consistently outputs $p_t < 0.05$ across the entire recording, maintaining an empty false alarm register.

### Figure F: Hardware Inference & Latency Benchmark
- **Testing Environment:** NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 with Max-Q Design (4 GB VRAM), PyTorch 2.6, CUDA 13.
- **Protocol:** Batch size $B=1$, Sequence length $T=16$, Image dimension $224 \times 224$, 25 warmup passes, 100 timed repetitions using `torch.cuda.Event` GPU timing.
- **Latency Distribution:**

## 4. Missing Experiments & Scientific Boundaries for the IEEE Paper

To adhere strictly to IEEE scientific integrity guidelines, the paper should explicitly acknowledge the following limitations:

1. **Staircase-Specific Fall Detection:**
   - *Status:* Missing / Unmeasured.
   - *Constraint:* The URFD dataset contains only flat-surface indoor living room and office falls. No staircase video footage exists in the current corpus. The authors **must not claim staircase fall detection performance** unless tested on dedicated datasets (e.g., Le2i Fall Detection Dataset staircase partition).
2. **True Geriatric / Elderly Population Representation:**
   - *Status:* Proxy benchmark caveat.
   - *Constraint:* In the URFD benchmark, fall actions were performed by healthy human volunteers (university students/researchers) simulating elderly falls onto protective floor mats. True geriatric falls frequently involve tremors, walking aids (canes, walkers), altered postural biomechanics, and involuntary neuromuscular collapse.
3. **Embedded Hardware Deployment (Raspberry Pi / Jetson Nano / Coral):**
   - *Status:* Tested on x86_64 Laptop GPU only.
   - *Constraint:* While throughput on the GTX 1650 was empirically verified at $32.6$ clips/s, claims regarding edge-compute performance on ARM-based SBCs (e.g. Raspberry Pi 4/5, Jetson Orin Nano, or Google Coral TPU) cannot be asserted without physical bench testing on those devices.
4. **Long-Duration False Alarm Rate per Day (FAR/day):**
   - *Status:* Clip-level validation only.
   - *Constraint:* While the model achieved 0 false alarms across the 66 validation ADL clips, true clinical monitoring requires 24-hour continuous streaming to report false positive alarms per 24 hours of operation (FAR/day).
